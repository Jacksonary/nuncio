Nuncio 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日

Nuncio 是一款在你电脑上运行的开源桌面应用，用于收发邮件、管理日历、联系人和待办事项。本政策说明 Nuncio 如何访问、使用、存储和共享你的数据。

1. 概要

  • Nuncio 没有自有服务端。应用直接连接你添加的邮件、日历服务商（如 Google、Microsoft、你的邮箱服务器）。
  • Nuncio 不收集遥测、使用统计或崩溃报告，不包含广告，不出售、出租或共享你的任何数据。
  • 你的邮件、日历、联系人、待办数据和账户凭据只保存在你自己的电脑上。

2. Nuncio 访问的数据

只有在你主动添加账户并授权后，Nuncio 才会访问该账户的以下数据：

数据用途
邮件（邮件头、正文、附件、文件夹/标签、已读等状态）在应用中显示、搜索、收发和整理你的邮件
日历和日程显示、创建、编辑日程，回复会议邀请，发送提醒
联系人显示和编辑通讯录，写邮件时自动补全收件人
待办任务显示、创建、编辑和完成任务
账户基本信息（邮箱地址、显示名称）识别和显示你添加的账户

2.1 Google 用户数据

当你添加 Google 账户时，Nuncio 通过 Google OAuth 申请以下权限：https://mail.google.com/（通过 IMAP/SMTP 收发邮件）、calendar（日历）、contacts 与 contacts.other.readonly（联系人与收件人补全）、tasks（待办），以及 openid、email、profile（识别账户）。

Nuncio 对通过 Google API 获取的信息的使用及向其他应用的传输，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”（Limited Use）要求。具体而言：

  • Google 用户数据只用于在你的设备上为你提供上述邮件、日历、联系人和待办功能；
  • 不会传输给任何第三方，不会用于广告，不会出售；
  • 不会被任何人工阅读（Nuncio 开发者无法访问你的数据）；
  • 不会用于训练任何人工智能或机器学习模型。

2.2 Microsoft 用户数据

当你添加 Outlook.com 或 Microsoft 365 账户时，Nuncio 通过 Microsoft 身份平台申请 IMAP、SMTP、日历、联系人、待办和基本资料等委托权限，用途与上表相同，同样只在你的设备上使用。

3. 数据存储与安全

  • 邮件、日历、联系人、待办数据缓存在你电脑上的应用数据目录中（本地 SQLite 数据库及邮件文件）。
  • 密码、授权码和 OAuth 令牌保存在操作系统的凭据存储中（Windows 凭据管理器、macOS 钥匙串、Linux Secret Service）；当系统凭据存储不可用时，使用加密的本地文件。
  • 与服务商的所有连接均使用 TLS 加密。
  • 应用日志只保存在本机，不包含密码、令牌或邮件正文。

4. 网络连接

Nuncio 只会发起以下网络连接：

  • 你添加的账户所对应的服务商（邮件服务器、Google API、Microsoft Graph、CalDAV/CardDAV 服务器）；
  • OAuth 登录时，打开 Google 或 Microsoft 的官方登录页面；
  • （可选，可在设置中关闭）添加账户时向 Mozilla Thunderbird 的公开配置数据库查询邮箱服务器设置，此时会发送邮箱的域名部分（不含用户名）；
  • （可选，可在设置中关闭）向 GitHub Releases 检查应用更新，失败时回退到 Gitee 镜像；
  • 你选择显示的邮件远程图片（默认拦截）。

5. 数据共享

Nuncio 不与任何第三方共享你的数据。除你自己选择发送的邮件、会议回复等内容外，你的数据不会离开你的设备与服务商之间的直接连接。

6. 你的控制权与数据删除

  • 在 Nuncio 中删除某个账户，会删除该账户在本机的全部缓存数据和保存的凭据。
  • 卸载 Nuncio 并删除其应用数据目录，即可清除所有本地数据。
  • 你可以随时撤销 Nuncio 的访问授权：Google 账户在 myaccount.google.com/permissions；Microsoft 账户在 account.live.com/consent/Manage（工作或学校账户在 myapps.microsoft.com）。

7. 儿童隐私

Nuncio 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 政策变更

如本政策有变更，将在本页面更新并修改生效日期。

9. 联系方式

如对本政策有任何疑问，请在 GitHub Issues 中提出。

Nuncio Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Nuncio is an open-source desktop application that runs on your computer to manage your mail, calendar, contacts and to-do lists. This policy explains how Nuncio accesses, uses, stores and shares your data.

1. Summary

  • Nuncio has no server of its own. The app connects directly to the mail and calendar providers you add (such as Google, Microsoft or your mail server).
  • Nuncio collects no telemetry, usage statistics or crash reports, shows no ads, and never sells, rents or shares your data.
  • Your mail, calendar, contacts, tasks and account credentials are stored only on your own computer.

2. Data Nuncio accesses

Nuncio accesses the following data only after you add an account and grant access:

DataPurpose
Email (headers, bodies, attachments, folders/labels, read state)Display, search, send, receive and organize your email in the app
Calendars and eventsDisplay, create and edit events, respond to invitations, show reminders
ContactsDisplay and edit your address book; suggest recipients while composing
TasksDisplay, create, edit and complete tasks
Basic account information (email address, display name)Identify and display the accounts you added

2.1 Google user data

When you add a Google account, Nuncio requests these OAuth scopes: https://mail.google.com/ (sending and receiving mail via IMAP/SMTP), calendar, contacts and contacts.other.readonly (contacts and recipient suggestions), tasks, and openid, email, profile (account identification).

Nuncio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the mail, calendar, contacts and to-do features described above, on your device;
  • it is not transferred to any third party, not used for advertising and not sold;
  • it is not read by humans (the Nuncio developers have no access to your data);
  • it is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model.

2.2 Microsoft user data

When you add an Outlook.com or Microsoft 365 account, Nuncio requests delegated permissions through the Microsoft identity platform for IMAP, SMTP, calendars, contacts, tasks and basic profile. The data is used for the same purposes as above, only on your device.

3. Storage and security

  • Mail, calendar, contact and task data is cached in the app's data folder on your computer (a local SQLite database and message files).
  • Passwords, app passwords and OAuth tokens are kept in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service), or in an encrypted local file when no system store is available.
  • All connections to providers use TLS encryption.
  • Application logs stay on your computer and never contain passwords, tokens or message bodies.

4. Network connections

Nuncio only connects to:

  • the providers of the accounts you added (mail servers, Google APIs, Microsoft Graph, CalDAV/CardDAV servers);
  • the official Google or Microsoft sign-in pages during OAuth sign-in;
  • (optional, can be turned off in Settings) Mozilla Thunderbird's public configuration database to look up mail server settings when adding an account — only the domain part of the address is sent;
  • (optional, can be turned off in Settings) GitHub Releases, falling back to a Gitee mirror, to check for app updates;
  • remote images in messages, only when you choose to show them (blocked by default).

5. Sharing

Nuncio does not share your data with any third party. Apart from content you choose to send (such as emails or meeting responses), your data never leaves the direct connection between your device and your providers.

6. Your choices and data deletion

  • Removing an account in Nuncio deletes all of that account's locally cached data and stored credentials.
  • Uninstalling Nuncio and deleting its data folder removes all local data.
  • You can revoke Nuncio's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for Microsoft personal accounts at account.live.com/consent/Manage (work or school accounts at myapps.microsoft.com).

7. Children's privacy

Nuncio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated together with the effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
